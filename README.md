# Smart Home App

## Installation:
1. Head over to the [apk](apk) folder and download the latest version of the app on your device
- Your browser may warn you that downloading an `.apk` file can be dangerous, so you'll have to confirm that you still want to download it
2. Open the `.apk` on your device
- Again, your device may warn you that installing an app through `.apk` files is dangerous, so confirm that you still want to open it
- You may have to give your browser permission to install apps.
3. Click "Install"
4. And there you have it, the app is installed!
